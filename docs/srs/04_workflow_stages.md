# Chapter 4: Statutory Acquisition Workflow Stages

The land acquisition lifecycle progresses through eight distinct, evidence-gated statutory stages:

```
[Stage 1: Initiate] ──> [Stage 2: Verify]  ──> [Stage 3: Notify] ──> [Stage 4: Resolve]
                                                                            │
                                                                            v
[Stage 8: Assure]   <── [Stage 7: Possess] <── [Stage 6: Deliver]<── [Stage 5: Award]
```

### Stage 1 - Initiate
- **Input**: Approved preliminary alignment corridor, requiring body authorization, budget sanction, applicable statutory regime (RFCTLARR 2013).
- **Actions**: Generate immutable project case identifier; assign state policy pack; import corridor centerline; generate candidate parcels.
- **Evidence Gate**: Requiring body mandate, geometric corridor validity, initial finance envelope.
- **Output**: Formal project baseline and candidate parcel inventory.

### Stage 2 - Verify
- **Input**: Authoritative State RoR, registration registers, cadastral vector polygons.
- **Actions**: Cross-reference recorded ownership, cadastral boundaries, and revenue court litigation; enumerate affected households.
- **Evidence Gate**: Automated cross-source confidence evaluation. Contradictions generate quarantined exceptions.
- **Output**: Verified parcel inventory and initial SIA survey universe.

### Stage 3 - Notify
- **Input**: Verified parcel boundaries.
- **Actions**: Freeze statutory geometry snapshot; publish Section 11 gazette notification; launch objection countdown clock (60-day statutory window).
- **Evidence Gate**: Gazette publication confirmation and digital proof of public disclosure.
- **Output**: Effective statutory notice active; objection channels opened.

### Stage 4 - Resolve
- **Input**: Public objections, court stay notices, heir/succession claims.
- **Actions**: Conduct formal hearings; record speaking orders; reconcile boundaries; update ownership without overwriting historical records.
- **Evidence Gate**: Speaking order signed by Competent Authority for each objection.
- **Output**: Legally cleared parcels ready for valuation.

### Stage 5 - Award
- **Input**: Land valuation inputs, asset enumeration, solatium factors, R&R entitlement matrix.
- **Actions**: Compute compensation totals; link R&R rehabilitation packages; verify no unmatched SIA households.
- **Evidence Gate**: Mandatory check: Zero unresolved title/area disputes and zero unmatched vulnerable households.
- **Output**: Signed award package and legal financial liability.

### Stage 6 - Deliver
- **Input**: Beneficiary bank accounts, PFMS payment instructions, Section 77 court deposit receipts.
- **Actions**: Dispatch electronic payments; capture bank acknowledgement; confirm beneficiary credit or lawful court deposit.
- **Evidence Gate**: Bank reconciliation verification (zero unacknowledged transactions).
- **Output**: Legally settled compensation record.

### Stage 7 - Possess
- **Input**: Field possession party deployment.
- **Actions**: Collect offline geotagged boundary photos; capture panchnama with village witness signatures; upload possession memorandum.
- **Evidence Gate**: Dual verification: Award compensation paid + physical field possession proof confirmed.
- **Output**: Verified physical possession; parcel marked construction-ready.

### Stage 8 - Assure
- **Input**: Post-possession monitoring, 3-month and 6-month R&R livelihood outcome tracking.
- **Actions**: Verify sustained resettlement outcomes; assemble immutable audit package.
- **Evidence Gate**: Formal handover protocol signed with infrastructure concessionaire.
- **Output**: Concessionaire corridor entry with zero unaddressed liabilities.
