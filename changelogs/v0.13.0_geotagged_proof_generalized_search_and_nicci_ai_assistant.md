# Changelog: v0.13.0 - Geotagged Document Proofs, Generalized Multi-Criteria Search, Optimized Scraper & NICCI Interactive AI Assistant

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Milestone:** v0.13.0  
**Focus Areas:** High-Fidelity Evidentiary Document Proofs, Sub-Meter DGPS Geo-Tagging, Multi-Criteria Corridor Discovery, Parallel C++ Scraper with SHA-256 Deduplication, Bitemporal Dataset Revisions, Explanatory Query Diagnosis, and Official "NICCI" AI Assistant with Voice Speech Synthesis & Speech-to-Text Recognition.

---

## 1. Summary of Changes

This release addresses six mission-critical capabilities requested for the DHARTI National Land Acquisition Control Plane (SIH 26016):
1. **Certified Evidentiary Document Proofs & DGPS Geotags**: Transcending bare cryptographic hashes by attaching authentic Government of India clearance letterheads, official digital stamps, Gazette S.O. citations, and sub-meter DGPS survey coordinates (Trimble R12 GNSS, WGS84, WKT cadastral boundaries, MSL elevation, chainage, and satellite visualizer).
2. **Generalized Multi-Criteria Corridor Discovery**: Liberating PARIVESH and Bhoomi Rashi querying from rigid proposal/reference numbers through multi-attribute tokenization across project names, highway identifiers (NE-7, NE-4, NE-5, NH-319B, NE-II), states (full name and code), districts, taluks, and clearance types.
3. **Scraper Optimization**: Implementing asynchronous multi-source polling via `std::async`, in-memory SHA-256 deduplication cache to eliminate redundant network traffic and Drive uploads, and cross-platform POSIX/macOS pipeline execution.
4. **Bitemporal Revision History & Multi-Corridor Dataset**: Ingesting rich multi-corridor dossiers across 5 national expressways (NE-7 Bengaluru-Chennai, NE-4 Delhi-Mumbai, NE-5 Delhi-Amritsar-Katra, NH-319B Varanasi-Ranchi-Kolkata, NH-NE-II Eastern Peripheral) with complete chronological transaction time ($T_t$) and valid time ($T_v$) delta tables.
5. **C++ Explanatory Query Engine**: Native diagnostic engine linking natural language queries to statutory provisions (RFCTLARR Act 2013 §38, §16, §31, §77; NH Act 1956 §3A, §3C, §3D, §3G; Constitution Art. 226) and actionable remediation steps.
6. **Official "NICCI" AI Assistant with Voice Narration**: Authentic government digital assistant modeled directly after the National Informatics Centre (NIC) Chat Interface:
   - "NEED HELP? ASK NICCI" floating pill callout and circular avatar of the friendly digital assistant with red glasses.
   - Onboarding splash screen with white Lion Capital State Emblem of India and name capture.
   - Active chat interface with sub-toolbar (speech toggle, clear, maximize, external link).
   - Real-time Web Speech Synthesis (Text-to-Speech) with visual soundwave speaking indicators.
   - Web Speech Recognition (Microphone Speech-to-Text) for hands-free voice querying.
   - Direct integration with DHARTI data, geotagged proof modals, and tab navigation.

## 2. Added Functions & Components

### C++ Backend Core
- `models::GeoLocation`: Geodetic survey struct containing latitude, longitude, elevation (MSL), chainage range, UTM zone, surveyor officer, DGPS device hardware IMEI, Bhuvan GIS reference, and WKT cadastral boundary polygon.
- `models::DocumentProof`: Evidentiary certificate struct encapsulating official letter numbers, Gazette S.O. references, issuing authorities, signatory credentials, digital signature hashes, issuance dates, statutory clauses, and Google Drive vault pointers.
- `WebScraper::search_corridors(query, state_filter)`: Generalized multi-criteria corridor search filtering across all corridors, state names, districts, taluks, and document proof metadata.
- `WebScraper::fetch_all_parallel(urls, timeout_sec)`: Asynchronous multi-threaded scraper leveraging `std::async` to query disparate government endpoints concurrently.
- `WebScraper::is_payload_changed(record_id, new_sha256)`: In-memory SHA-256 cache preventing duplicate storage ingestion and redundant network payloads.
- `services::ExplanatoryQueryEngine`: Pure C++ statutory diagnostic service generating root-cause diagnoses, continuous frontage impacts, statutory act citations, and CALA remediation steps.
- CLI Flags in `main.cpp`:
  - `--search <query>`: Discovers corridors by name, highway, state, or district without requiring proposal IDs.
  - `--explain <query>`: Produces statutory diagnosis and frontage gain analysis.
  - `--show-proof <project_id>`: Displays verified official evidentiary document proof with DGPS survey specs.

### Web Frontend & UI
- `DocumentProofModal` (`web/js/document_proof_modal.js`): High-fidelity official parchment modal with Indian Government letterhead, Ashoka emblem, DGPS accuracy badge, interactive satellite mini-map, DGPS surveyor hardware metadata, CALA digital seal, and RFC 6234 SHA-256 Drive vault link.
- `NicciAssistant` (`web/js/dharini_assistant.js`): Official NIC digital assistant interface with floating pill callout, splash screen, sub-toolbar, millisecond timestamps, SpeechSynthesis voice narration, and microphone speech-to-text.
- `ExplanatoryEngine` (`web/js/explanatory_engine.js`): Client-side knowledge engine diagnosing bottlenecks, judicial court stays, SIA vulnerable families, and 7-rule gate quarantines.
- `web/data/live_scraped_evidence.json`: Comprehensive 5-corridor national dataset containing 142.0 km of alignment data, DGPS survey coordinates, and bitemporal revision logs.
