# Release v0.5.0: Modular Architecture, Federated Adapters & Bitemporal Event Store

**Release Date**: 2026-09-05  
**Milestone**: Full Modular Domain Architecture, Federated Adapters, Bitemporal Ledger & End-to-End Walkthrough  
**Target Specification**: SIH 26016 / Smart India Hackathon 2024  

---

## 1. Summary of Changes

This release fulfills the user mandate for a completely modular, pure C++17 architecture with side-by-side feasibility and effectiveness analyses, modular folder-based documentation (`docs/srs/`, `changelogs/`, `docs/analysis/`), and zero redundant Python code. It introduces the federated adapter subsystem, bitemporal event store, financial reconciler, statutory workflow coordinator, and executes the complete SIH 26016 4-minute demo scenario.

---

## 2. Added

### 2.1 Architectural & Legal Analysis (`docs/analysis/`)
- **`01_architecture_feasibility_and_effectiveness.md`**:
  - Evaluation of constitutional federalism (Seventh Schedule, List II Entry 18) proving why a federated control plane is constitutionally viable while a centralized land registry replacement is legally infeasible.
  - Formal effectiveness analysis of the 7 non-negotiable invariants.
- **`02_pci_algorithm_complexity_and_benchmarks.md`**:
  - Mathematical formulation of Possession Continuity Index (PCI).
  - Algorithmic analysis of $O(M \log M)$ contiguous interval merging and $O(K \cdot M \log M)$ unlock simulation.
  - Performance benchmarks demonstrating sub-millisecond execution ($< 0.5 \text{ ms}$ for 10,000 intervals in C++17).
- **`03_social_safeguards_and_legal_compliance.md`**:
  - Compliance analysis with RFCTLARR Act 2013 (Sections 4-9, 15, 19, 23, 31-38) and World Bank Environmental & Social Standard 5 (ESS5).
  - Algorithmic specification of the "No Family Invisible" rule protecting non-titleholder livelihood dependents.
- **`index.md`**: Structured catalog of all analysis documents.

### 2.2 Federated Ingestion Adapters (`include/dharti/adapters/` & `src/adapters/`)
- **`adapter_interface.hpp`**:
  - Abstract base interface `IAdapter` with deterministic FNV-1a / SHA-256 provenance hash generation, UTC timestamping, and schema quarantine logic.
- **`land_record_adapter.hpp` / `.cpp`**:
  - Ingestion and spatial harmonization of State Record of Rights (RoR) and Cadastral GIS Vector Geometries.
  - Generates immutable provenance hashes and quaratines malformed records.
- **`court_adapter.hpp` / `.cpp`**:
  - Ingestion and judicial encumbrance tracking for eCourts CIS and State RCCMS revenue court dockets.
  - Determines active stay status and tracks vacate orders.
- **`finance_adapter.hpp` / `.cpp`**:
  - Ingestion of Public Financial Management System (PFMS) payment instructions and bank UTR credit confirmations.
  - Classifies failed transactions with standard banking failure codes.

### 2.3 Core Bitemporal Event Store (`include/dharti/core/` & `src/core/`)
- **`event_store.hpp` / `.cpp`**:
  - Thread-safe, append-only bitemporal event ledger implementing Invariant 1 (Zero Silent Overwrites) and Invariant 7 (Bitemporal Auditability).
  - Cryptographic hash chaining verifying 100% unbroken sequence and payload integrity.
  - Dual-axis historical queries: transaction-time ($T_t$) and valid-time ($T_v$).

### 2.4 Application Services (`include/dharti/services/` & `src/services/`)
- **`payment_reconciler.hpp` / `.cpp`**:
  - Tracks statutory compensation obligations and reconciles against PFMS bank settlements.
  - Enforces Invariant 3: Blocks physical possession until payments are verified settled.
- **`workflow_coordinator.hpp` / `.cpp`**:
  - Orchestrates the 8 statutory stages (Initiate, Survey, Inquire, Award, Compensate, Dispute Resolution, Possess, Handover).
  - Multi-agency exception resolution (judicial stays, succession conflicts, payment re-dispatch).
  - Corridor-wide PCI evaluation and unlock ranking.

### 2.5 Verification & Demo
- **`tests/cpp/test_main.cpp`**:
  - Added unit tests for all 3 federated adapters, event store integrity, payment reconciliation.
  - Implemented the complete 4-minute demo scenario: 12 km corridor, 5 parcels, P-118 identified as bottleneck, live multi-agency resolution, and PCI jump from 41.7% to 83.3%!
