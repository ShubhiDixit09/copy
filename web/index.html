📖 मार्गदर्शिका एवं वैधानिक शब्दावली | Statutory Terminology & Mathematical Engine Guide

Official statutory explanation of how DHARTI unifies multi-ministry land acquisition laws, prevents corrupt compensation claims, and eliminates highway construction bottlenecks.

1. Project Continuity Index (PCI) / सतत फ्रंटेज

Measures continuous usable highway frontage. Having 80% parcels acquired does not allow road laying if plots are separated by court stays. A minimum 5.0 km uninterrupted run is required for heavy equipment deployment.

2. Section 3A vs 3D (Bhoomi Rashi) / धारा 3A एवं 3D

Section 3A: Notification of intention to acquire land for public purpose.
Section 3D: Final statutory declaration. Upon 3D publication, land vests absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.

3. Cryptographic SHA-256 Provenance / साक्ष्य प्रमाण

Every PDF gazette and environmental order is hashed using RFC 6234 SHA-256 at the exact microsecond of fetch. Hashes are recorded in Neon DB; files are locked in Google Drive. Neither can be forged or retroactively altered.

4. 7-Rule Evidence Gate / 7-नियम साक्ष्य गेट

An automatic circuit breaker. If an incoming scraper payload has a broken checksum, conflicting survey numbers, or an impossible area jump (>300%), it is quarantined into Neon DB exceptions for Human-in-the-Loop review.
